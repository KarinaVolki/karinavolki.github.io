K-Samira Content Studio

A desktop tool for connecting the owner's TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit and uploading original local vertical videos as drafts through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in through TikTok's official OAuth consent screen.
  2. The creator selects an original local video file.
  3. The app uploads the video to the creator's TikTok account as a draft for review and publishing.

The app does not use browser automation, passwords, cookies, scraping, or unofficial TikTok endpoints. It does not access or publish content belonging to other TikTok users.
