Privacy Policy

This policy explains how K-Samira Content Studio processes information when the owner connects a TikTok account and uploads original local videos through TikTok's official APIs.

Information processed

With your permission through TikTok Login Kit, the application receives the basic profile information needed to identify the authorized creator account. During an upload, it processes the local video file selected by you and the upload status returned by TikTok.

TikTok authorization data

OAuth authorization data, including access and refresh tokens, is used only to maintain the authorized connection and make permitted API requests. It is stored locally by the application and is not displayed in the user interface or sent to unrelated third parties. Keep your computer and local account secure.

How information is used

The application uses the information only to authenticate the owner's TikTok account, identify that account, upload the owner's selected video as a draft, and display the resulting status.

Sharing and retention

The application does not sell personal information and does not use browser automation, passwords, cookies, scraping, or unofficial TikTok endpoints. TikTok receives information necessary to provide the authorized API features under TikTok's policies. Local application data remains until you delete it or revoke the connection.

Your choices

You may stop using the application, delete local application data, or revoke access through TikTok. For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the official website.
